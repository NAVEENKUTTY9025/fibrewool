Artisan Millwork

The Journey from Fleece to Yarn

Trace the transparent, ethical, and meticulous process behind our premium, small-batch yarn production.

Step 01

Sourcing & Shearing

We select only the finest fleeces, working hand-in-hand with regional family farms committed to ethical animal husbandry and regenerative land stewardship.

  • Ethical Partnership: Direct sourcing from local families, ensuring fair compensation and shearing practices.
  • Humane Shearing: Conducted strictly in spring by certified shearers to prioritize sheep comfort.
  • Rigorous Sorting: Fleece is hand-skirted and categorized by micron width to guarantee superior softness.
Farm Origin Regional Family Farms
Certification Humane Shearing Certified
Fleece Grade Extra Fine (< 20 microns)
Step 02

Washing & Carding

Raw fleece undergoes a gentle wash to clean debris while preserving the natural properties of the fibers, followed by carding to align them into fluffy rovings.

  • Eco-friendly Wash: Using zero harsh chemicals and organic, biodegradable soaps.
  • Water Conservation: Closed-loop water filtration system reclaiming up to 70% of wash water.
  • Artisan Carding: Drum-carding that maintains the light, airy volume of wool fibers.
Soap Type 100% Biodegradable Organic
Water Reclaim Rate 70% Filtration & Reuse
Roving Quality Ultra-lofty & Uniform
Step 03

Spinning & Plying

Using heritage spinning techniques, we spin the rovings into single strands, then ply them together to achieve balanced, strong, and highly durable yarn.

  • Heritage Craft: Custom ring-spinning that preserves the natural crimp and insulation.
  • Optimal Strength: Multi-plying options crafted for robust stitch definition and wear.
  • Quality Checks: Ongoing laser diameter measurement for exceptional thickness consistency.
Spin Technology Traditional Ring Spinning
Plies Produced 2-ply / 3-ply / Aran
Diameter Consistency 99.2% Uniform Grade
Step 04

Natural Dyeing

Our small-batch botanical dyeing uses hand-gathered herbs, roots, and minerals to infuse each batch with deep, earthy color profiles that reflect our local landscape.

  • Botanical Pigments: Hand-crafted dye pots using indigo, madder root, weld, and walnut hulls.
  • Metal-Free Mordants: Safe, non-toxic organic compounds that bind colors permanently.
  • Small-Batch Variety: Hand-dyed in small copper kettles, ensuring unique artistic color play.
Dye Material 100% Plant & Mineral Base
Vat Method Traditional Copper Kettles
Carbon Footprint Neutral Small-Batch Process
