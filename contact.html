Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Do you offer custom dyeing?
Yes! We offer small-batch custom dyeing for studios and shops. Minimum order 5 lbs. Contact our wholesale team for a color consultation.
Can I visit the mill?
Absolutely! Our studio is open for tours Wednesday-Saturday. Reserve a time via our booking form or just drop by during open hours.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship worldwide. Rates calculated at checkout. Free shipping on US orders over $100.
What's your return policy?
Unused, unopened yarn can be returned within 30 days. Custom dye lots are final sale.
